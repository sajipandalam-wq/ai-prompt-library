# Diwali Video From 1 Photo (100% free)

**Post:** *Just 1 photo ➜ a beautiful Diwali video. No camera, no shooting.*

1. **Gemini:** upload your photo and create Diwali scenes.
2. **Google Flow → Frames to Video:** add camera movement.
3. At the end of every prompt, add: `Keep his face identical`
4. Join the clips with a fire transition and add music.

For camera-movement words, see [cinematic-camera-words.md](cinematic-camera-words.md).
