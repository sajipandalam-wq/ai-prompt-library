# Swap People in a Viral Video With Your AI Characters

**Post:** *Same scene. New faces. One prompt.*

## Steps

1. Pick a reference video.
2. Make your 2 AI character photos.
3. Open Higgsfield Genjutsu (or try Google Flow).
4. Upload the video and your 2 photos.
5. Paste the prompt below and generate.

## Prompt

```
Use the uploaded video only as a motion reference. Transfer all movements, actions, timing, body language, gestures, and camera motion from the video to @image1 and @image2. Replace the original people completely with the characters from @image1 and @image2, including both their faces and bodies. Maintain accurate identity, proportions, clothing, and appearance from the reference images throughout the entire video. The final result should look as if the people from @image1 and @image2 are naturally performing all the actions shown in the original video, with smooth, realistic motion and consistent identity from start to finish.
```

**Tip:** use clear, front-facing character photos for the best result.
