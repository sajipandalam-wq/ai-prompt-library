# Video Camera-Move Prompts (Kling / Google Flow)

Add one camera move per clip. Keep clips 5 to 10 seconds long.

| Move | Prompt snippet |
|---|---|
| Dolly in | `slow dolly in toward the subject's face, shallow depth of field` |
| Orbit | `smooth 180-degree orbit around the subject, steady speed` |
| Crane up | `camera cranes up from ground level to reveal the city skyline` |
| Tracking | `side tracking shot following the subject walking, parallax background` |
| Whip pan | `fast whip pan to the right, motion blur transition` |
| Push through | `camera pushes through the doorway into the bright room` |
| Drone pull-back | `aerial drone pulls back and rises, revealing the full landscape` |

## Full example

```
The man walks along Dubai Marina at sunset, side tracking shot following him, warm golden light, gentle breeze in his shirt, cinematic 24fps, realistic motion, 9:16, 8 seconds.
```
