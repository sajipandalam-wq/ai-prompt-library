# The "Sandwich" Face-Lock Technique

AI tools often drift away from your real face. The sandwich method puts the identity instruction **at the start and the end** of the prompt, with the creative scene in the middle.

## Structure

```
[TOP SLICE]   Use the uploaded photo as the exact face reference. Keep identical facial features, face shape, skin tone, hairstyle and beard.
[FILLING]     <your scene, outfit, lighting, style, camera>
[BOTTOM SLICE] Do not change the face. The face must match the reference photo 100%. Photorealistic, natural skin texture.
```

## Tips

- Use a sharp, well-lit, front-facing selfie with no filters.
- If you wear spectacles in real life, say so: `wearing thin dark half-rim spectacles`.
- Avoid heavy stylisation words such as "anime" or "Pixar" when you need a true likeness.
- Regenerate rather than over-editing one result.
