# My Claude Video Edit Rules (rules.md)

These are the standing rules I give Claude when it edits my reels. Save this as `rules.md` in the same folder as your raw clips. Then start every edit with:

```
Here's my clip and my rules.md. Build a storyboard first. Don't render until I approve.
```

To make it yours, change the handles, voice name and end card, and add a line whenever you catch yourself giving the same note twice.

# SS Creative Studio — Edit Rules
Brand: SS Creative Studio · "AI • Art • Anointing" · by Saji Pandalam

## Format
- Vertical 9:16, 1080 x 1920, 30fps.
- Shorts/Reels: 60 seconds maximum. If the script runs longer, split it into Part 1 / Part 2 and tell me where the split falls.
- Never repost or re-cut an old video with a new hook. Every reel is a new topic.

## Voice & audio
- Keep my voice exactly as recorded. If I ask for a revoice, use my Higgsfield cloned voice "saji_1".
- Voiceover is Malayalam or English (I'll say which). Don't translate my script unless I ask.
- Soft, matching sound effects on key moments (pops, whooshes, swipes). No music unless I ask.
- Level the audio so the voice is always clear over the SFX.

## On-screen text & captions
- On-screen text and captions are in ENGLISH ONLY, even when the voiceover is Malayalam.
- Bold, stylish, eye-catching type, placed in the lower part of the frame.
- Never cover my face or the avatar's face, and never block the main subject of the scene.
- Captions: 1 to 3 words at a time, timed to my voice.
- Key lines get a bigger animated caption placed in front of the avatar (lower centre), not off to the left or right.

## Visual pace
- A new visual, cut, zoom or effect every 2 seconds. No dead air, no empty frames.
- First 3 seconds = the hook. Big text, motion, no slow intro or logo opener.
- For tool tutorials: show real screenshots, real logos and real screen recordings. Never invent features, prices or numbers.
- Recommend FREE tools and free routes first (e.g. Google Flow instead of paid tools). Most of my audience uses free versions.

## Layouts I use
- Talking avatar full screen with captions below.
- Split screen: demo/product video on top, my avatar on the bottom.
- Devotional reels: calm pacing, warm light, scripture reference shown clearly on screen.

## Ending (every reel)
- Close with a save + follow ask, NOT a "comment" ask. Spoken sign-off: "veendum kaanam, bye".
- End card (2–3 seconds): SS Creative Studio logo, "Saji Pandalam" under the brand, and the handles:
  - Instagram @sajipandalam
  - TikTok @sajipdmdxb
  - YouTube @sajipdm
  - Facebook facebook.com/sajipandalam
- Hashtags for the caption (not on screen): #SSCreativeStudio #AIArtAnointing #sajipdm

## Workflow (most important)
1. Show me a storyboard/preview with screenshots of each beat first.
2. Before showing me, render a contact sheet of every beat, check it yourself, and fix anything cut off, cluttered, misspelled or covering a face. List what you fixed.
3. Do NOT render the final video until I approve.
4. When I give notes, apply them all in one pass.
